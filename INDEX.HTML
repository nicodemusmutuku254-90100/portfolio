MERU UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

Meru university of science and technology started in the year 2013

Meru university of science and technology offers following programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
  • Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Mathematics
  • Bachelor of Science in Physics
  • Bachelor of Science in Chemistry

    • The university is located in Meru, nchiru (main campus), Kenya.
    University is a public university that is accredited by the commission for university educationin kenya
